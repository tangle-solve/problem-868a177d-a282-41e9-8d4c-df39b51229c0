### CHEM-000300: Long heparan-sulfate chains with independently programmed epimers and sulfates
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Specify every uronate epimer and N-, 2-O-, 3-O- and 6-O-sulfation site in long chains, preserving the pattern through synthesis and purification.
- **Status:** A 2023 platform produced all 64 N-/2-O-/6-O-sulfation patterns on a fixed tetrasaccharide framework. Long-chain epimer and 3-O-sulfate programming remain outside that comprehensive library.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Enables causal tests of long-range sulfation domains.
- **Key works:** L. Wang et al. (2023), *Efficient platform for synthesizing comprehensive heparan sulfate oligosaccharide libraries for decoding glycosaminoglycan–protein interactions*, [DOI:10.1038/s41557-023-01248-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-023-01248-4).
- **Related:** Computational biology: sequence–function inference for heterogeneous polymers.
